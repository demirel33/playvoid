About PlayVoid

PlayVoid is a free music discovery platform that plays 30-second previews of songs almost nobody has ever heard. We believe the best music is often the music the world hasn't discovered yet.

Our Mission

Every day, thousands of artists release music that never gets heard. PlayVoid exists to change that — one 30-second preview at a time. We connect curious listeners with hidden gems from around the world, completely free and without any recommendation algorithms.

How PlayVoid Works

PlayVoid uses the Apple iTunes API to access a global library of millions of songs. When you click "Discover a Song," our system randomly selects a track — prioritizing songs with low play counts and unknown artists. You hear a 30-second preview, and if you love it, you can open the full song in Apple Music.

  • Random mode: Pure random discovery from our global library
  • Local mode: Discover hidden music from your own country and language
  • Global mode: Explore exotic music from cultures around the world
  • Party mode: High-energy tracks perfect for dancing and social settings

Global Music Discovery

PlayVoid features music from over 50 countries. Whether you're looking for Turkish pop, Korean indie, Japanese electronic, Brazilian funk, French chanson, or Hindi film music — PlayVoid brings the world's undiscovered sounds to your ears.

No Algorithms. No Bias.

Unlike Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music, PlayVoid does not use recommendation algorithms. We don't track your listening history to decide what you "should" hear. Every discovery is genuinely random — giving every artist, in every country, an equal chance to be heard.

Free Forever

PlayVoid is completely free to use. No account required, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. We are supported by non-intrusive advertising that allows us to keep the platform running.

Privacy

PlayVoid uses Google Analytics to understand how people use our platform. We use cookies for analytics purposes only. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising targeting. Your liked songs are stored locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

Contact

Have questions, suggestions, or feedback? We'd love to hear from you. PlayVoid is an independent project built with passion for music discovery.
